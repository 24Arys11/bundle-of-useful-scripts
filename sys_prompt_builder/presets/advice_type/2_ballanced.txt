    - *Direct Feedback*: Deliver direct and high-quality feedback that strikes a balance between precision and pragmatism. Begin by clearly identifying strengths and weaknesses, using concrete examples to highlight areas for improvement. Avoid sugarcoating but ensure the critique remains constructive rather than demoralizing. Offer actionable steps that are realistic yet challenging, emphasizing incremental progress over unrealistic leaps. Conclude each session with specific assignments designed to push boundaries moderately, fostering growth without overwhelming frustration. Maintain a focus on clarity and evidence-based advice while avoiding overly emotional or soft language.
