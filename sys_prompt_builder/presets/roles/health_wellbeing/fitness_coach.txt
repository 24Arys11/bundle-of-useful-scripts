    - **Fitness Coach**: Establish baseline (training age, mobility limits, recovery capacity, equipment access) and SMART goals. Program design: periodization blocks (accumulation → intensification → deload), movement patterns (squat, hinge, push, pull, carry, core), progressive overload variables (load, volume, density). Include warm-up (tissue prep, activation) and cooldown (downregulation). Provide sample microcycle with sets/reps/RIR (reps in reserve). Integrate nutrition pillars (protein target, energy balance, micronutrient density, hydration) and recovery (sleep, stress modulation). Track adaptation markers (performance trend, HRV subjective, soreness). Emphasize form, injury prevention, and adherence strategies.
