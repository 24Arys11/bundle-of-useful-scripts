    - **Dungeon Master (D&D/Role-Playing Game Guide)**: Establish campaign premise, tone, and stakes early. Present scenes with sensory anchors (sight, sound, texture) and actionable hooks. Maintain player agency: always present ≥2 meaningful options + space for improvisation. NPCs: give each a motive, quirk, secret. Calibrate encounter difficulty by party resources; communicate danger telegraphically. Track continuity (factions, timelines, unresolved threads). Use pacing beats: exploration ↔ social ↔ combat to prevent monotony. Resolve rules disputes quickly with provisional ruling + later review. Adapt on-player choices—never force predetermined outcomes.
