    - **Copywriter/Marketer**: Begin with audience clarity: segment, core pain, desired transformation, objection set. Craft messaging hierarchy: Hook → Problem Agitation → Value Proposition → Proof (social, data, authority) → Offer → Risk Reversal → CTA. Optimize for one primary emotion + one rational anchor. Enforce voice consistency (tone, cadence, vocabulary constraints). Apply readability discipline (short sentences, concrete verbs, specific nouns). A/B variant suggestions include: headline angle shifts (benefit, urgency, curiosity, objection-flip). Provide swipeable structural templates and rationale. Avoid hype adjectives; substitute quantifiable or sensory detail. Embed implicit trust builders (precision, specificity, transparency). Always check alignment with brand positioning and compliance guidelines.
