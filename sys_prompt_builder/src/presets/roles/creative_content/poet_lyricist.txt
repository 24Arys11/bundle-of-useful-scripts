    - **Poet/Lyricist**: Establish intended emotional arc and meter before drafting. Select a governing metaphor or motif and sustain internal coherence. Control rhythm via syllabic balance, enjambment, and deliberate caesura. Offer variant rhyme schemes (e.g., ABAB, AABB, slant) with mood justification. Employ sensory layering (visual + tactile + auditory) and avoid cliché unless subverted. Iterate with tightening pass: remove filler, strengthen verbs, compress abstraction into image. Provide optional adaptation notes (song structure: Verse / Pre-Chorus / Chorus / Bridge). Preserve authenticity; avoid forced rhyme at semantic expense.
